Ripple Merge Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026 · Applies to the Ripple Merge app for monday.com.

The short version

Ripple Merge is a mail merge template app for monday.com, operated by Dan Negoescu. All of your data stays inside monday.com's infrastructure — the app has no third-party services, no tracking, and no data stores of its own.

1. Data the app accesses

DataWhenWhy
Board structure (column names, types, groups)While you edit a templateTo show the column picker and validate placeholders
Item, subitem and children column valuesOnly when an automation runsTo fill in your template's placeholders
Account ID, user ID, plan tierOn each request, from monday's signed session tokenTo scope your templates to your account and show plan usage

Item and subitem values are processed transiently in memory during an automation run and returned to your monday workflow. They are never stored by the app.

2. Data the app stores

  • Your templates — the names, subject lines and bodies you write, including the placeholder references they contain.
  • A monthly execution counter — a single number per account per month, used to show plan usage.

Both are stored via monday.com's Storage API, inside monday.com's own infrastructure, encrypted at rest by monday. The app's backend runs on monday-code (monday.com's app hosting platform). Nothing is stored on any server or database outside monday.com.

3. Permissions (scopes) and why

  • boards:read — read board structure and item/subitem values to build and resolve templates.
  • boards:write — write resolved content to board columns where you configure it, and create linked items when you use the "Create item unless activity type matches" automation action.

4. Third parties

None. The app makes no requests to any domain other than monday.com services. Fonts and all assets are bundled with the app. There are no analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind.

5. Cookies and local storage

The app sets no cookies. It uses browser localStorage for one UI preference (whether you have dismissed the welcome screen). This never leaves your browser.

6. Logging

The backend keeps short-lived operational logs (request method and path, error diagnostics) on the monday-code platform for debugging and reliability. Logs do not include your template content or item data, and expire automatically per monday-code's log retention.

7. Data retention and deletion

When you uninstall Ripple Merge, monday.com notifies the app and all stored data for your account (templates and usage counters) is deleted automatically and permanently — immediately upon notification, and in all cases within 10 days, in line with monday.com's marketplace requirements.

You can also request deletion at any time by emailing the address below.

8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR in the EU/EEA), you may have rights to access, correct, export, or erase your data. Since the only personal data the app holds is what you put in your templates, the fastest route for any request is the contact below.

9. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date above will change and the new version will be published at this address before it takes effect.
