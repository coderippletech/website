Built for monday.com

Mail merge for monday.com,
subitems included.

monday's email automations can only pull columns from a single item. So the subitem rows get pasted in by hand. Ripple Merge puts them in, with loops, totals and formatted dates, then hands the finished email to monday to send.

Reach the data monday's emails can't

Build the template once. Every send fills itself in.

Subitem data in emails

Put subitem columns in the body with {subitem.Description}, or pull one row with {subitems[0].Description}. monday's native emails can't reach this data.

Click to insert, no syntax

Click any column in the picker and it lands in your template as a placeholder. Nothing to memorize, nothing to mistype.

Loops & filters

List every invoice line: {#each subitem} repeats a block for each row. Add a filter like where Status = "Paid" to keep only the rows you want.

Totals & smart dates

Sum, average, count, min or max across subitems, like {subitem.Line Total | sum}. Format any date column with 13 presets, from 31st March 2026 to 03/31/2026.

Plain text or rich HTML

Send plain text, or switch to HTML for bold, lists, links and on-brand formatting. You choose per template.

Templates across boards

See every template on every board in one account-wide workspace view. Search them, edit them, copy one to another board.

From template to sent email in four steps

Two on the board, two in the workflow builder.

1

Add the view

On the board you send from: + Add view → Apps → Ripple Merge.

2

Build your template

Click + New, then click columns to insert placeholders. Choose Plain Text or HTML.

3

Add the action

In the workflow builder, add "Ripple Merge — Resolve Template" after your trigger and pick your template.

4

Chain Send Email

Add monday's Send Email step. Map Subject to Resolved Subject and Message to Resolved Content.

No new email sender to trust

Ripple Merge resolves the template. Then monday's own Send Email action does the sending.

  • Works with the triggers you already use
  • Returns Resolved Subject and Resolved Content for the next step
  • Add as many Resolve + Send pairs as one workflow needs

Your templates stay in your monday account

Templates live in monday's Storage API, and the backend runs on monday-code, monday's own hosting platform. The only outside service is OpenAI, and only when you use an AI feature.

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking
  • Item and subitem values are processed in memory during a run, never stored
  • AI features stay off until you use them. The privacy policy says exactly what they send
  • Uninstall the app and its stored data is deleted automatically
monday.com · Ripple Merge

Or describe the email and AI writes the template

Ripple Merge AI turns a sentence into a ready-to-send mail merge template, then keeps working at send time.

Write with AI

Describe the email, like "order confirmation listing every line item with a total, friendly tone". You get a complete template with placeholders matched to your actual board columns, including subitem loops, totals and formatted dates. Empty board? The AI suggests three templates to start from.

A fresh summary in every email

Drop {ai.summary} into a template and every email opens with a 2–3 sentence AI-written summary of the item and its line items. It's generated from live board data the moment your automation fires.

Your client's language, automatically

Point a template at a Country or Language column and each email is AI-translated into the recipient's language at send time. "Spain" is enough. Translations are cached and placeholders stay intact.

Refine anything

More formal, friendlier, shorter, grammar-perfect, or translated into six languages. One click, and every placeholder survives the rewrite. AI subject-line suggestions included.

Self-healing templates

Renamed or deleted a column? Templates flag it. "Fix columns with AI" remaps every broken placeholder onto your real columns in one click, and you review before saving.

Private by design

The editor AI sees only your column names and what you type — never board item values. The opt-in AI summary and language settings are the only send-time exceptions, processed transiently and fully disclosed in the privacy policy.
