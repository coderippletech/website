Everything monday's email automations can't do

Build the template once. Every send fills itself in.

Subitem data in emails

Reference subitem columns right in the body — {subitem.Description} — or pull one row with {subitems[0].Description}. monday's native emails can't reach this data.

Click to insert, no syntax

Click any column in the picker and it lands in your template as a placeholder. Nothing to memorize, nothing to mistype.

Loops & filters

{#each subitem} repeats a block for every row — perfect for invoice lines. Add a filter like where Status = "Paid" to keep only the rows you want.

Totals & smart dates

Sum, average, count, min or max across subitems — {subitem.Line Total | sum}. Format any date column with 13 presets, from 31st March 2026 to 03/31/2026.

Plain text or rich HTML

Send simple text, or switch to HTML for bold, lists, links and on-brand formatting. Your choice per template.

Templates across boards

An account-wide workspace view lists every template on every board. Search them, edit them, copy one to another board.

From template to sent email in four steps

Set it up once. Every automation email fills itself in.

1

Add the view

On the board you send from: + Add view → Apps → Ripple Merge.

2

Build your template

Click + New, then click columns to insert placeholders. Choose Plain Text or HTML.

3

Add the action

In the workflow builder, add "Ripple Merge — Resolve Template" after your trigger and pick your template.

4

Chain Send Email

Add monday's Send Email step. Map Subject to Resolved Subject and Message to Resolved Content.

Drops straight into your automations

No new email sender to trust. Ripple Merge resolves the template, then monday's own Send Email action does the sending.

  • Works with the triggers you already use
  • Returns Resolved Subject and Resolved Content for the next step
  • Add as many Resolve + Send pairs as one workflow needs

Your data never leaves monday.com

Templates live in monday's Storage API. The backend runs on monday-code, monday's own hosting platform. There is nothing else.

  • No third-party services, no analytics, no tracking
  • Item and subitem values are processed in memory during a run, never stored
  • Uninstall the app and its stored data is deleted automatically
