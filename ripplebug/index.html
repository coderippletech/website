Record the bug on
the phone it happened on.

The Ripplebug app for iPhone and Android records any app while you reproduce the bug, or takes the screenshot you already have. It files to the same board, with the phone's model and OS attached.

  • Trim the recording and mark the moment it broke
  • Arrows, boxes and blur, right on the screenshot
  • Spots matching cards, so nobody files the same bug twice
  • No signal? Reports wait in an outbox and send when you're back
  • A notification when your report moves or gets a reply

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. Your own website can carry the report button too: embed.js captures the console, network and steps in any phone browser.
